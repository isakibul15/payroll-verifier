⚠ The Problem
BPO Payroll is a Compliance Minefield
Business Process Outsourcing firms process millions of sensitive financial documents yearly — without standardised AI governance.
📄

Massive Document Volume

Expense receipts, payroll slips, tax forms — at industrial scale, far beyond what human reviewers can handle accurately or consistently.

⚖️

EU AI Act — High Risk

Since August 2024, payroll and tax AI is legally High-Risk. Every AI decision must be traceable, explainable, and auditable by law.

🔓

Uncontrolled PII Exposure

Typical AI pipelines send IBANs, names, and home addresses to cloud LLMs in plaintext — a GDPR violation at the architectural level.

⚠ Difficulties
Why Existing Solutions Fall Short
  • ✗No audit trail — AI decisions are black boxes. Article 12 requires full traceability for every output.
  • ✗PII sent to cloud LLMs in plaintext — violates GDPR Data Minimisation at the pipeline level.
  • ✗No human override mechanism — mandatory for high-risk AI under Article 14.
  • ✗Zero plain-language explanations — employees cannot understand or appeal decisions (Article 86).
  • ⚠OCR, policy checks, and tax slabs live in separate disconnected systems — no unified compliance layer.
  • ⚠Manual escalation is slow, inconsistent, and leaves no verifiable paper trail.
